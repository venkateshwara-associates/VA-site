FAQ

Common questions

Do you guarantee loan approval?

No. Every application goes through an internal credit assessment based on your business turnover, documentation, and repayment capacity. We only confirm terms in writing once an offer is sanctioned.

Are there any upfront or advance fees?

We do not charge any advance/upfront payment to approve or disburse a loan. Any applicable processing fees are disclosed clearly before you sign an agreement, and are deducted from or billed alongside the sanctioned loan — never collected in advance to "unlock" funding.

How is the interest rate decided?

Interest rates depend on the loan amount, tenure, and your business's risk profile, and are shared with you in writing as part of the sanction offer before you agree to proceed.

What documents are typically required?

Common requirements include business registration proof, recent bank statements, KYC of the proprietor/partners/directors, and basic financial/turnover details. We'll confirm the exact checklist for your case during the enquiry step.

What if I face repayment difficulty?

Contact us as early as possible — we'd rather work out a revised plan with you than let an account fall into default. Our contact and grievance details are on the Contact page.
