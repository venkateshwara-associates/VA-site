Our Services

Loan & funding options for growing businesses

Every offer we make is confirmed in writing before you sign anything — clear interest rate, fees, and repayment schedule, no hidden charges, no advance payments.

💼

Short Term Business Loans

Unsecured funding designed for immediate business needs:

  • Working capital for day-to-day operations
  • Expansion, inventory, or equipment purchase
  • Short tenures aligned to your cash flow cycle
  • Repayment structured around your turnover
📈

High Value Private Funding

Larger structured funding for established businesses:

  • Funding from ₹25 Lakhs up to ₹50 Crores
  • Sized to your business turnover and financials
  • Suited for expansion, acquisition, or large working-capital needs
  • Custom repayment structuring based on cash flows
🧾

Documented Approval Process

A process built to be faster and clearer than typical bank timelines:

  • Minimal paperwork, clearly listed upfront
  • Assessment based on turnover and repayment capacity
  • Written sanction terms before any agreement is signed
  • No advance fees to "process" or "approve" your loan
📋

Typical Documents Required

Exact requirements are confirmed per case, but commonly include:

  • Business registration / Udyam or GST proof
  • Last 6–12 months' bank statements
  • KYC of proprietor/partners/directors (ID & address proof)
  • Basic financial statements or turnover details
📄

Custom terms, not fixed rate cards. Because interest rates, fees, and eligible amounts depend on your business turnover, tenure, and risk profile, we don't publish a generic rate card. Share your requirement with us and we'll come back with a clear, written offer — there's no cost or obligation to enquire.
