Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Venkateshwara Associates ("we", "us", "our"), a Udyam-registered proprietary firm based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu (Udyam Reg. No. UDYAM-TN-02-0334094), collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website or apply for a business loan or funding through us.

1. Information We Collect

When you enquire or apply for funding, we may collect information such as your name, business name, phone number, email address, funding requirement, and — if you proceed with an application — supporting documents such as business registration proof, bank statements, KYC identity/address proof, and basic financial or turnover details.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and assess eligibility for funding
  • To carry out credit assessment and prepare sanction terms
  • To prepare loan agreements and manage disbursal and repayment
  • To communicate updates related to your application or account

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Documents and financial information you share are used solely for assessing and servicing your funding request.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not share your information with third parties except where necessary to process your application (for example, verification checks) or where required by law. Any such sharing is limited to what is necessary for that purpose.

4. Data Retention & Security

We retain applicant and borrower information for as long as necessary to service your loan and to meet our legal and record-keeping obligations, and take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

5. Cookies & Analytics

This website may use minimal, privacy-respecting analytics to understand overall site usage. We do not use cookies for cross-site advertising or tracking.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have shared with us (subject to our legal record-keeping obligations for active or past loan accounts) by contacting us at venkateshwaraassociates.office@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
